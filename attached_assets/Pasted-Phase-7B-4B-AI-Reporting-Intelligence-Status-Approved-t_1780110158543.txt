Phase 7B-4B — AI Reporting Intelligence

Status: Approved to Begin

Objective

Transform Ora Reporting from a report-export system into an executive-level analytical system capable of generating management-quality insights, business impact assessments, prioritization, and strategic recommendations.

This phase must enhance intelligence only.

Do NOT modify:

* DOCX generation architecture
* XLSX generation architecture
* PPTX generation architecture
* PDF generation architecture
* Export menu architecture
* Template picker architecture
* AI Builder
* Developer Mode
* Plan Mode
* Knowledge Vault
* Web Search
* Image Generation

These systems are considered stable baselines.

⸻

PRIMARY DELIVERABLES

Add AI-generated reporting intelligence:

1. Overall Health Score (0–100)

Generate:

* Overall Health Score
* Health Category

Categories:

90–100 = Excellent
75–89 = Good
60–74 = Needs Attention
40–59 = High Risk
0–39 = Critical

Must be based on:

* KPI gaps
* Trend performance
* Risks
* Action-plan volume
* Operational findings

Return both score and explanation.

⸻

2. Financial Impact Assessment

Generate:

* Estimated cost of current issues
* Estimated savings opportunity
* Estimated revenue opportunity
* Estimated waste reduction opportunity

If exact data unavailable:

Use AI estimation ranges and clearly mark:

Low Confidence
Medium Confidence
High Confidence

Never fabricate exact financial numbers without supporting evidence.

⸻

3. Operational Impact Assessment

Generate:

* Productivity impact
* Throughput impact
* Downtime impact
* Labor impact
* Quality impact
* Capacity impact

Return:

Low
Moderate
High
Critical

With explanation.

⸻

4. Customer Impact Assessment

Generate:

* Customer experience impact
* Service impact
* Delivery impact
* Product quality impact
* Reputation impact

Return severity and explanation.

⸻

5. Why This Matters Section

New executive narrative section.

Explain:

* Why leadership should care
* Consequences of inaction
* Strategic implications
* Competitive implications

Write at executive level.

6. Expected Benefits

For every recommendation:

Generate:

* Expected benefit
* Expected timeline
* Estimated difficulty
* Confidence level

Example:

Recommendation:

Expected Benefit:
4–8% throughput improvement

Timeline:
30–60 days

Difficulty:
Medium

Confidence:
High
