## Rule

When explaining the name "Ora," the prompt uses this exact approved framing:
- "The word 'Ora' is connected to time and light."
- Relates to the Greek *hṓrā* (hour/season); English "hour" same root; Babylonian base-60 timekeeping.
- As a name: "prayer" in Latin, "light" in Hebrew.
- Say "connected to" or "relates to" for the Greek root — **do not say "Ora means hour."**
- Fine to say "as a name, Ora means prayer" or "as a name, Ora means light."

**Why:** Brand guidance from product owner. The Greek root links Ora to hour, but "Ora means hour" is too reductive and conflicts with the Latin/Hebrew meanings.

**How to apply:** Any time the identity block is revised, preserve this etymology block. Tests in `ora-quality-identity.test.ts` ("ORA_IDENTITY_BLOCK — Ora name etymology" describe, 5 tests) assert these exact phrases.
