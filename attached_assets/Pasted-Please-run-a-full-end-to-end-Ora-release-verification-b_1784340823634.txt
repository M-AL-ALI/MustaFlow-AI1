    * Plan/tier sync matches website.
    * Talk to Ora for 10+ turns.
    * Search/current info.
    * Image generation/editing.
    * Upload/read files and generate/export files.
    * Conversation history sync.
    * Settings/account deletion flow.

9. Submit a final report with:
