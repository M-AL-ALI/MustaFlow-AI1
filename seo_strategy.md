# SEO Strategy

## In scope

- Public marketing and discovery routes: `/`, `/gallery`, `/extensions`, `/community`, `/u/:username`
- Public information and trust routes: `/pricing`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/help`, `/help/domains-api`, `/status`, `/trust`, `/developers`, `/developers/changelog`
- Platform-level public publishing surface: `/api/p/:slug/*` and custom-domain snapshot serving when behavior is controlled by platform source code

## Out of scope

- Authenticated product routes such as `/projects/**`, `/ora/**`, `/settings`, `/billing`, `/admin/**`, `/workspaces/**`
- Internal preview and staging routes such as `/api/p/:slug/staging/*` and `/api/p/:previewSlug/preview/*`
- User-generated page content inside individual published snapshots except where the platform injects or rewrites SEO-critical HTML

## Target audience

- Non-technical users who want to describe, build, and publish apps with AI
- Secondary audience: developers evaluating templates, extensions, and platform capabilities

## Primary keywords

- AI app builder
- no-code app builder
- AI website builder
- app templates
- extensions marketplace
